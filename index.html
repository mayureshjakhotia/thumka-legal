Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Thumka works without an account. There is no sign-up, no login, no profile, and no server holding your videos. Thumka does not ask for your name, email, or phone number. This policy explains the little data that does exist, and where it goes.

Your videos never leave your device

Every video you import — from your photo library, your files, a share from another app, or a direct link — is stored locally in Thumka's own storage on your phone. So is everything you do with it: notes, loop sections, practice status, practice time, and your streak. Thumka has no server to upload any of it to. We never see, receive, or store your videos or your practice data.

What we do collect

DataWhat it isLinked to you?

Alongside them we record four coarse properties: whether you are on the free or paid tier, roughly how many videos are in your library, your streak length, and your chosen theme. Reading it involves a Firebase installation identifier, which Google generates for the app on your device. No. It identifies an installation, not a person, and it is not combined with anything else.
Purchase records Whether Pro is active on this device. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play and recorded by RevenueCat, our billing provider, under an anonymous ID that RevenueCat generates itself (it looks like $RCAnonymousID:…). Thumka does not choose or supply that ID and does not attach an email or any other detail to it. Not by us. Apple or Google knows the purchase belongs to your store account, because they took the payment. RevenueCat sees only the anonymous ID and the store receipt.
Free-import count and app-scoped device ID A single number: how many videos have ever been imported on this device. It exists so the five free imports cannot be reset by deleting and reinstalling the app. On iOS the count stays on the device. On Android, Thumka sends the app-scoped Android device ID with a Google Play Integrity token to our quota service. The service immediately converts the ID into a one-way keyed value and stores only that value, the count, and random retry IDs — never the raw device ID, video, filename, or URL. Not to your name or contact details. The Android record distinguishes one device for fraud prevention, and Google verifies that the request comes from the genuine Play-installed app on a recognized device.

We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never share it with anyone except the two processors above (Firebase/Google and RevenueCat), strictly to run the app. Thumka does not use Apple's Advertising Identifier or Android's Advertising ID, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, because we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Support requests

Thumka has no ticket system and stores no email address. When you tap Email support in Settings, Thumka opens a draft in your own email app, pre-filled with your app version, your platform, and the anonymous billing ID described above — the last of these is the only way to find a purchase that has gone wrong, since there is no account to look you up by. Nothing is sent automatically. You can read the draft, edit it, delete any of those lines, or discard it. If you do send it, we then have your email address in our inbox, in the ordinary way that emailing someone gives them your address.

Payment information

All purchases go through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Thumka never sees or stores your card number, billing address, or any payment details — the stores handle that entirely.

Your choices

  • Delete your videos and practice data: Settings → Delete Library & Practice Data, any time, right on your device. This removes the videos stored on the device along with your notes, loop sections and practice history. It does not cancel a purchase, and it does not reset the free-import count.
  • Turn off analytics: Settings → Privacy. Nothing further is collected after that.
  • Stop future collection: delete the app. On Android, the one-way device quota record remains so reinstalling cannot reset the free allowance.
  • Ask about data already collected: email us (below). Records already held by Apple, Google, RevenueCat or Firebase live in those companies' systems, not inside Thumka, so the app itself cannot reach in and erase them — but tell us what you need and we will make the request we are able to make.

Children

Thumka isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, we'll update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.

Your content stays yours

Videos you import remain entirely your property. Thumka claims no rights over them — they're stored locally on your device and we never access them.

The free allowance

Thumka is free to use for your first five video imports on a device. Imports, not videos: the count goes up when an import succeeds and never comes back down, so deleting a video does not return an import. A cancelled or failed import doesn't count, and the sample clip Thumka ships with doesn't either. The count is kept locally on iOS and by Thumka's protected quota service on Android, and is not reset by reinstalling. Pro removes the limit.

This is a permanent free allowance, not a trial. Nothing expires and nothing starts billing you on its own.

Purchases

  • Monthly auto-renews until cancelled, billed through your Apple ID or Google account.
  • Lifetime and Founding Lifetime are one-time purchases that unlock Pro permanently — they don't renew and can't expire.

  • Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies — Thumka doesn't process billing directly and can't issue refunds itself.
  • The Founding Lifetime price is offered to a limited number of early members and may be withdrawn for new purchases at any time; it never affects anyone who already bought it.

Common questions

Does my dance video ever leave my phone?
No. Everything is stored locally in the app. There's no server to upload to.
Do I need an account?
There isn't one. No sign-up, no login, no password, nothing to lose access to.
I bought Pro on Android. Do I get it on my iPhone too?
No. Purchases are specific to the store you bought from, because Thumka has no account to carry them across. An Android purchase unlocks Pro on Android; an iPhone purchase unlocks Pro on iPhone.
I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?
Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, then Settings → Restore purchases. You won't be charged again. This works between devices on the same platform.
How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Thumka → Cancel. Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Thumka → Cancel. It stays active until the current billing period ends.
I bought Lifetime — will I ever be charged again?
No. Lifetime and Founding Lifetime are one-time purchases with no renewal.
How do I get my five free imports back?
You can't, and that's deliberate. The count is tied to the device and never goes down, so deleting videos, clearing app data or reinstalling won't reset it. Pro removes the limit entirely.
How do I delete my videos and practice data?
Settings → Delete Library & Practice Data. It removes the videos on the device plus your notes, loop sections and practice history. Your purchase is unaffected, and the free-import count stays where it is.
How do I request my analytics data be deleted?
Email jakhotiamayuresh@gmail.com and tell us what you need. Data held by Apple, Google, RevenueCat or Firebase sits in their systems rather than inside Thumka, so the app can't erase it directly — we'll make the request we're able to make on your behalf.
