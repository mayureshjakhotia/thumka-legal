Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Thumka is built to work without an account. There's no sign-up, no profile, and no server holding your videos. This policy explains the little data that does exist, and where it goes.

Your videos never leave your device

Every video you import — from Photos, Files, a shared link, or a URL — is stored locally in the app's own storage on your phone. Thumka has no server to upload them to. We never see, receive, or store your videos.

What we do collect

DataWhat it isLinked to you?
Usage & feature events Which features you use (slow-motion, mirror, loop, AI step breakdown), screen views, app opens — via Firebase Analytics. No. No name, email, or account — just an anonymous device install.
Diagnostics Crash reports and device/OS/app-version info, via Firebase Crashlytics — helps us fix bugs. No.
Purchase status Whether you've subscribed or bought Lifetime access, handled by RevenueCat and Apple's App Store. Tied to your device's purchase history via Apple, not to your identity by us.

We don't use this data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone except the two processors above (Firebase/Google and RevenueCat), strictly to run the app. Thumka does not use Apple's Advertising Identifier and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, because we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Payment information

All purchases go through Apple's App Store. Thumka never sees or stores your card number, billing address, or any payment details — Apple handles that entirely.

Your choices

  • Delete your videos: Settings → Delete all videos, any time, right on your device.
  • Delete or export your analytics data: email us (below) and we'll process the request.
  • Turn off tracking entirely: delete the app — nothing is retained on any server tied to you, since nothing was ever sent to one under your identity.

Children

Thumka isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, we'll update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.

Common questions

Does my dance video ever leave my phone?
No. Everything is stored locally in the app. There's no server to upload to.
How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Thumka → Cancel. It stays active until the current billing period ends.
I bought Lifetime — will I ever be charged again?
No. Lifetime and Founding Lifetime are one-time purchases with no renewal.
How do I get my free 5 videos back / reset my trial?
The free limit is tied to your device, by design, so it can't be reset by reinstalling the app. Subscribing or buying Lifetime removes the limit entirely.
How do I delete all my videos?
Settings → Delete all videos.
How do I request my analytics data be deleted?
Email support@thumka.app — we'll process it promptly.
