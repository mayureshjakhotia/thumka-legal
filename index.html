Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Thumka works without an account. There is no sign-up, no login, no profile, and no server holding your videos. Thumka does not ask for your name, email, or phone number. This policy explains the little data that does exist, and where it goes.

Your videos never leave your device

Every video you import — from your photo library, your files, a share from another app, or a direct link — is stored locally in Thumka's own storage on your device. Your imported video content, filenames, loop boundaries, AI pose results, and exact practice history are not uploaded to Thumka, Google Analytics, RevenueCat, or another analytics service. When you import a direct link, Thumka contacts that address and any redirect hosts only to download the video, so those hosts receive the normal network request; Thumka does not send the link to its own server or an analytics service. The current iOS release does not upload product analytics. On Android, Google ML Kit performs pose detection on the device; Google documents that ML Kit sends limited operational information described below, not the video or detected pose.

What we do collect

DataWhat it isLinked to you?
On-device activity log The current iOS and Android releases do not configure Firebase product analytics. Thumka keeps a small, bounded and sanitized recent-activity log on your device so an issue report can say which screen or feature was in use. It is written only after an app action, not from video frames, camera frames, playback timers, or a background network job. It stays on your device unless you deliberately attach and send it in an issue report. It contains no account, advertising identifier, billing identifier, filename, path, URL, video identifier, media, pose result, exact playback position, or free-form error.
Diagnostics The current iOS and Android releases do not use Firebase Crashlytics and do not automatically upload Thumka crash stack traces. On Android, Google ML Kit may collect device and app information, performance metrics, API configuration, feature input and output sizes, event types, and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics when AI Steps pose detection is used. Google says this data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. Apple or Google may also provide aggregated store diagnostics under their own privacy controls. ML Kit uses per-installation identifiers not intended to identify a person or physical device. The optional Thumka support attachment is associated with the email you choose to send; it is not sent in the background and can be removed before sending.
Configuration Thumka's product settings are bundled with the app. The current iOS and Android releases do not configure Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or product Remote Config. Google documents that Android ML Kit Pose Detection internally uses Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations for remote-configuration diagnostics. This limited ML Kit configuration data is associated with an app installation, not a Thumka account, name, email, advertising identifier, or video.
Purchase records Whether Pro is active on this device. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play and recorded by RevenueCat, our billing provider, under an anonymous ID that RevenueCat generates itself (it looks like $RCAnonymousID:…). Thumka does not choose or supply that ID and does not attach an email or any other detail to it. Not by us. Apple or Google knows the purchase belongs to your store account, because they took the payment. RevenueCat sees only the anonymous ID and the store receipt.
Free-import count A single number records how many videos have been imported on the device. It is stored locally on both iOS and Android and may also be included in the device's ordinary app backup. Thumka does not send an Android ID, Apple device identifier, or Play Integrity token to a quota service. It is local app state, not linked to a name, account, contact detail, or advertising identifier.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising. RevenueCat processes purchase status only to provide and restore Pro access. Thumka disables RevenueCat's automatic device-identifier collection and diagnostics. Thumka does not use Apple's Advertising Identifier or Android's Advertising ID, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, because we do not track you across other apps or websites. Google's current ML Kit disclosure is available at developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

Support requests

Thumka has no ticket system and stores no email address in the app. When you tap Report an issue by email in Settings, Thumka opens an editable draft in your chosen email app. The draft includes your app version and, when available, an anonymous billing reference that lets us find a purchase problem. It also offers a small text attachment with app/device status, current thermal and Low Power state, and a bounded list of recent app-owned screen or feature events. The attachment never includes your videos, filenames, paths, URLs, photo identifiers, billing or installation identifiers, or raw analytics. Nothing is sent automatically. You can read or edit the draft, remove the attachment, or discard the report. If you send it, we receive your email address and the material you chose to include in the ordinary way that emailing someone gives them your address. We use support reports only to answer and diagnose the issue, not for advertising or tracking.

Payment information

All purchases go through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Thumka never sees or stores your card number, billing address, or any payment details — the stores handle that entirely.

Your choices

  • Delete your videos and practice data: Settings → Delete Library & Practice Data, any time, right on your device. This removes the videos stored on the device along with your notes, loop sections and practice history. It does not cancel a purchase, and it does not reset the free-import count.
  • Control support diagnostics: the support message and attachment are visible before sending. You can edit the message, remove the attachment, or discard the report.
  • Stop local diagnostics: Delete Library & Practice Data removes the current local activity log, and deleting the app removes its remaining local data subject to your device's backup settings.
  • Ask about purchase or support data: email us below. Store and RevenueCat purchase records live in those companies' systems. An email report lives in the sender's and recipient's email systems.

Children

Thumka isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, we'll update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.

Common questions

Does my dance video ever leave my phone?
No. Everything is stored locally in the app. There's no server to upload to.
Do I need an account?
There isn't one. No sign-up, no login, no password, nothing to lose access to.
I bought Pro on Android. Do I get it on my iPhone too?
No. Purchases are specific to the store you bought from, because Thumka has no account to carry them across. An Android purchase unlocks Pro on Android; an iPhone purchase unlocks Pro on iPhone.
I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?
Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, then Settings → Restore purchases. You won't be charged again. This works between devices on the same platform.
How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Thumka → Cancel. Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Thumka → Cancel. It stays active until the current billing period ends.
I bought Lifetime — will I ever be charged again?
No. Lifetime and Founding Lifetime are one-time purchases with no renewal.
How do I get my five free imports back?
The local count never goes down when you delete a video. An ordinary device backup may restore it when the app is restored. Pro removes the limit entirely.
How do I delete my videos and practice data?
Settings → Delete Library & Practice Data. It removes the videos on the device plus loop sections, AI Steps, local diagnostics and practice history. Your purchase is unaffected, and the free-import count stays where it is.
Does Thumka upload analytics?
The current iOS and Android releases do not run Firebase product analytics. Their small app-owned diagnostic timeline stays on the device and leaves only if you deliberately include it in an issue report. On Android, the Google ML Kit SDK used for on-device pose detection sends limited installation, configuration, performance, event, and error diagnostics as described above; it does not receive your video or pose results.
